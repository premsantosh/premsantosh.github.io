Dessert this time, and not a common one. I wanted to try something new instead of the usual cake or ice cream route, so I roasted peach halves until they got some char, whipped mascarpone with vanilla, and plated them with a piece of honeycomb and crushed pistachios on top.

The nice thing about this one is that it's not very sweet. There's no added sugar in it. The sweetness comes from the peach itself, which gets more concentrated as it roasts, and from the honeycomb, which you break into as you eat. The mascarpone is the indulgent part since it's basically all fat, but a small amount carries the whole plate. Nutrition-wise it holds up better than most desserts: peaches bring vitamin C, vitamin A, and fiber, and pistachios add protein, healthy fats, and the crunch the soft fruit needs. I wouldn't call it health food, but as desserts go it's on the lighter end, and it tastes like more effort than it actually took.
