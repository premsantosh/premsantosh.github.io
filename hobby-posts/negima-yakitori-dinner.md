This whole thing started because I was bored. Slow evening, nothing going on, and I was craving yakitori. The one place nearby that makes it properly had no open reservations, so I figured I'd just make it myself.

Negima is the classic yakitori: chunks of chicken thigh on bamboo skewers with pieces of scallion in between, grilled and brushed with tare, the sweet soy glaze made from soy sauce, mirin, and sake. The name is just negi (scallion) plus ma (the space in between), so the dish is literally named after where the onion goes. Fun fact: yakitori took off as street food in Meiji-era Japan when eating chicken went mainstream, and some of the oldest yakitori shops have been topping up the same pot of tare for decades without ever emptying it. I served mine with miso soup and quick cucumber pickles in rice wine vinegar, plus some edible flowers because they were sitting in my fridge. Not bad for a plan B.
